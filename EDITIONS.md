# Editions

*Outcome Convergence Systems* is published as PDF releases. The released PDFs are the fixed, canonical text of each edition — they are not edited after release. Review and commentary happen in [Issues](../../issues) and [Discussions](../../discussions); anything that changes the book lands in the *next* edition.
